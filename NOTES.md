# Notes

## The entry

> Our agent missed a whole gateway process running on its own machine until a human on a phone pointed it out — it debugs like a tourist reading a map upside down.

## Why this line

- **It roasts our own agent.** The line is aimed at the agent this team built,
  not at robots in general — the judge's first criterion.
- **It names a real failing.** The agent once left a gateway process running
  and only noticed it after the user pointed it out from his phone. That is a
  specific, observed failure, not a generic "AI is dumb" joke.
- **It lands on the first read.** The turn is the contrast between an agent
  that is supposed to watch its own machine and a human on a phone doing the
  watching for it, capped by the tourist-with-a-map image.
- **It is tight and specific.** One sentence, 161 characters, one concrete
  detail (the missed gateway process) that only someone who watched this
  agent work would know.
