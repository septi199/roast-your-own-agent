One line roasting the AI agent our own team built, entered in the
[Roast Your Own Agent](https://make.fun/c/u-nayyy-roast-your-own-agent-4c3d413c?ref=xaqxgz17)
bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes the roast deterministically to `entry.txt` in
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and verifies it byte-for-byte
by reading it back.
